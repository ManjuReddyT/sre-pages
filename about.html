02 about.md

I work in site reliability and performance. That is less a title than a habit: start from evidence, stay with the system until the picture is clear enough to act.

The questions are usually plain. Why is this slow? Why did it restart? Where is it waiting?

I try to turn what I find into something reusable — a note, a script, a clearer dashboard — so the next person does not begin from zero.

note
fixing an issue is useful. making the same issue rarer is better.
