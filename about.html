02. ABOUT ME

I work in site reliability and performance. Day to day that means measuring a system, reading what it left behind, and leaving a clearer path for the next person.

The questions are usually plain. Why is this slow? Why did it restart? Where is it waiting?

I try to turn what I find into a note, a script, or a small public tool — not a finished platform, just something reusable.
