01. ABOUT

From performance testing to production reliability

I am a Site Reliability and Performance Engineer with 13 years of experience on large-scale distributed systems. The work started in performance testing and moved into production investigation, JVM diagnostics, and reliability engineering.

The useful part is turning that loop into practice and tools: understand behaviour under load, find why it failed, then make the next diagnosis cheaper.
