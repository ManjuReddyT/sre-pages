02. ABOUT ME █

I’m an SRE and performance engineering professional with 13 years of experience in building, testing, and improving software systems.

My work brings together performance engineering, application diagnostics, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, monitoring, and automation. I enjoy asking the difficult questions behind production issues: Why is the application slow? What caused the restart? Where is the bottleneck?

I like going beyond symptoms to understand the underlying behaviour of systems and turn those learnings into practical solutions. I’m also exploring how AI and automation can make engineering investigations faster and more useful.
