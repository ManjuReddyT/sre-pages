When the same diagnosis
repeats, it becomes a tool.

I work on reliability and performance of large distributed systems — load, latency, JVM runtime, and Kubernetes health. Thirteen years of turning production evidence into a clear next step.

Most incidents are not mysterious. They are a missing correlation between metrics, logs, a dump, and what the platform was doing at that moment.

My practice is evidence first: traces, JFR, heap and thread dumps, GC behavior, probe failures, and capacity under sustained load — before any change in production.

The public work here is the reusable part of that practice: courses for production debugging, and tools that cut the time between “something is wrong” and “here is the cause.”
