A short note

Most incidents are not mysterious. They are a missing join between metrics, logs, a dump, and what the platform was doing at that moment.

The working rule is simple: collect evidence first. Traces, JFR, heap and thread dumps, GC behaviour, probe failures, and capacity under sustained load — before any production change.

What you will find on this site is the reusable part of that practice. Courses for production debugging. Tools that shorten the path from “something is wrong” to “here is the cause.”
